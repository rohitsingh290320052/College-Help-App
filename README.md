# 📚 College Help App  

### Empowering Students with Seamless Assistance 🚀  

The **College Help App** started as a small campus startup 💡, aiming to solve everyday student challenges. Over time, it has evolved into a comprehensive platform that helps students rent items, access fast delivery services, share notes, and build a thriving community through real-time group chats.  

---

## ✨ Features  

### 1. **🎒 Rental Supply Platform**  
- Rent a wide range of items conveniently from within the app.  
- Manage rentals with ease through intuitive user interfaces.  

### 2. **⚡ Fast Delivery**  
- Get products delivered swiftly to your hostel.  
- Real-time updates on your orders and delivery status.  

### 3. **📖 Notes Sharing**  
- Share and access notes for academic purposes.  
- Streamline studying with a centralized repository of study materials.  

### 4. **💬 Group Up with like minded people**  
- Find people for your ideal hackathon team.  
- Chat Anonymously .    

### 5. **👤 User Profile**  
- View past orders and rental history.  
- Join the College Help Team through your profile options.  

---

## 🔧 Technologies Used  

### **Frontend**  
- **JAVA**: Ensures a smooth and intuitive user interface.  
- **Android SDK**: Provides the core building blocks of the application.  

### **Backend**  
- **Firebase Authentication**: Secures user login and registration.  
- **Firestore Database**: Manages real-time data for chats and orders.  
- **RoomDatabase**: Efficiently stores product and consumer details locally.
